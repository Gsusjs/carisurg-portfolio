# Urgency Tier Message Templates

Four urgency tiers, mapped from the underlying ESI prediction: Critical (ESI 1), High (ESI 2), Medium (ESI 3), Low (ESI 4 to 5). Each message is 12 words or fewer, uses active voice, and includes a clear action verb.

| Tier | Message | Word Count |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | CRITICAL: Assess this patient immediately. | 5 |
| High | HIGH: Assess this patient within 10 minutes. | 7 |
| Medium | MEDIUM: Assess this patient within 1 hour. | 7 |
| Low | LOW: Assess this patient during routine review. | 7 |

## Design Rationale

All four messages use the same verb and object, "Assess this patient", so the primary difference in urgency comes from the timeframe and tier label rather than differences in phrasing.

The timeframe progression, Immediately, 10 Minutes, 1 Hour, Routine Review, gives four distinct categories rather than two similar-sounding windows. The Low tier uses "routine review" rather than informal phrasing such as "no rush", to avoid implying the patient can be deprioritised entirely.
