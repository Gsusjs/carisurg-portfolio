# Peer Testing Plan

## Method

At least three cohort members will be recruited as testers. Each session will run in two parts.

### 1. Words Alone

Testers are shown only the **action and timeframe** portion of each message, with the tier prefix removed. For example:

> Assess this patient immediately.

Messages are presented in random order, with no colour attached.

Testers are asked:

> **"Which of these patients should you attend to first?"**

They are then asked to rank the remaining three messages in order and explain their reasoning aloud.

This isolates whether urgency can be judged from the **action and timeframe wording alone**.

### 2. Words With Tier Label and Colour

The same four messages are shown again with their **tier name** (`CRITICAL`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`) and **tier colour** attached, as they appear in the prototype.

Testers answer the same question a second time and state whether their interpretation of any message changed.

This structure separates two questions:

1. Does the action and timeframe wording alone communicate urgency correctly?
2. Does adding the tier label and colour together help or interfere with that interpretation?

## Data Collection

### Tier-Order Accuracy

Tier-order accuracy is recorded for each tester in each round.

Accuracy is calculated as the **percentage of the four tiers placed in their correct position** relative to the intended order:

**Critical → High → Medium → Low**

Results should be recorded separately for:

* Words alone
* Words with tier label and colour

### Qualitative Feedback

Verbatim notes will be captured during each session, including:

* What each tester got right
* What each tester misread
* Reasoning given for their rankings
* Any wording changes suggested by testers

### Post-Session Questionnaire

After the session, testers complete a short **System Usability Scale (SUS)-style questionnaire**, adapted to assess:

* Clarity of the urgency messages
* Confidence in ranking urgency
* Ease of distinguishing between tiers

### Targeted Open Question

Testers will also answer:

> **"Which message would you act on slowest, and why?"**

This targets the specific failure mode in this week's scenario: a message being interpreted as **lower urgency than intended**.

## Deliverables for Final Submission

The final submission will include:

* Verbatim feedback from all three or more testers.
* Tier-order accuracy for each tester, comparing **words alone** with **words with tier label and colour**.
* The tier most commonly misranked when words are shown alone.
* Whether adding the tier label and colour corrected or introduced any misreadings.
* Any wording changes made directly as a result of testing, with **before-and-after text** shown.
